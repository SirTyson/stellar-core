## Meta-Patterns

1. **Host Object Budget Charging Is Order-Sensitive**: `VisitObject` budget charging is interleaved with object validation in the Soroban host. Batching charges breaks the metering contract because it would either over-charge on error or under-charge before validation. Do not propose batch-charging patterns for host object visits.

2. **Parallel Thread State Setup Is Sub-Threshold (Transactions View)**: Like the soroban subsystem finding, serial `ThreadParallelApplyLedgerState` construction has been evaluated from the transactions subsystem angle and also found below threshold. The dominant cost in the parallel apply zone is worker execution, not setup.

3. **Fee Pool Is a Shared Mutable Resource**: The ledger header `feePool` field is incremented by every fee-processing call. Any parallelism in `processFeesSeqNums` requires either atomic header mutations or a reduce step, which adds complexity and likely more overhead than the saving. Fee processing parallelism is architecturally constrained.

4. **XDR Buffer Costs Are Distributed Below Threshold**: Multiple hypotheses have targeted XDR buffer allocation for footprint entries, rent computation, and bridge serialization. Each targets a different call site and each is individually sub-threshold (1–2.5%). These do not combine linearly since they are in different phases; a single combined refactor covering all of them might reach Medium but no single-site fix does.

5. **Wasmi InstancePre Is Store-Owned and One-Shot**: Confirmed from the transactions subsystem: wasmi `InstancePre` is constructed inside a specific store, owns allocated instance state, and consumes itself on initialization. It cannot be cloned, cached, or shared across invocations. This is consistent with the transaction-ledger subsystem finding; do not repropose wasmi instance-template caching from any angle.

6. **Auth Vector Semantic Non-Emptiness**: Source-account Soroban auth vectors in soroswap carry real router root invocation and SAC transfer sub-invocation data that must be decoded, metered, and matched against the call stack. No bridge flag or credential-type fast-path can skip this work without breaking authorization correctness for production soroswap shapes.

7. **Validation Zone Tracy Trap (Transactions Angle)**: Global `commonValidPreSeqNum` and `checkValid` self-time in apply-load traces is dominated by TX-set construction and flood validation, not by the in-apply path. The in-apply validation overlap is too small to reach Medium severity. Always timestamp-filter against `applyLedger` before projecting optimization impact on validation zones. This trap also applies to the sequential apply path (`applySequentialPhase`/`applyTransaction`/classic `TransactionFrame::apply`): when `APPLY_LOAD_CLASSIC_TXS_PER_LEDGER=0`, all classic transaction apply events in the trace come from pre-benchmark setup ledgers. Verify `applyTransaction.counts / applyLedger.counts` against configured classic TPS; if the ratio is much larger than the configured value, the bulk of apply-zone events are outside the measured window.

8. **Worker Aggregate Must Be Divided by Cluster Count**: When the soroswap workload runs at T=8, all per-worker Tracy zone totals (storage reads, map builds, frame pushes, ledger-change extraction) are aggregate across 8 concurrent lanes, not serial apply time. Always divide T-thread aggregate totals by T to obtain a critical-path upper bound before assessing severity. This pattern caused multiple reviewer-stage rejections (cluster-local storage reuse, frame rollback undo logs, mutable storage map writes, contract-instance frame caching, ledger-change map diff).

9. **Pre-Parallel-Apply Phase Is Thin**: The combined `preParallelApply` → `requiresSequentialPreParallelApply` phase and the `applyParallelPhase` bundle-build loop are each under ~2% of soroswap close time. Micro-optimizations in this serial phase (precomputing hashsets, hoisting header loads) cannot reach Medium severity without a future trace showing substantially higher overlap.

10. **addReads toCxxBuf Is Not a Soroswap Bottleneck**: InMemorySorobanState read-side re-encoding (`toCxxBuf` in `addReads`) is structurally redundant but not on the soroswap critical path. Total `addReads` self-time is 149ms across the full trace; even the most optimistic pre-serialization yields ≤11ms after T=8 division. Write-side bottlenecks (bulk-build storage maps, old-entry XDR sizes) have already received targeted fixes; remaining read-side overhead is sub-threshold. Size XDR payloads before proposing pre-serialization — 36-byte TTL entries cannot amortize cache infrastructure.

11. **Stage DAG Scheduling Overhead Can Exceed Barrier Gain**: A correctly implemented cross-stage dependency-DAG scheduler that eliminates per-stage barriers may still regress soroswap because: (1) the benchmark typically has one dominant Soroban stage, so there are no independent later-stage clusters to pipeline; (2) footprint-conflict computation, condition-variable infrastructure, and apply-thread dispatch add overhead that is not offset by the saved barrier-idle time. Always measure cluster-count distribution and stage-count variance on the target workload before implementing a scheduler redesign.

12. **HostMap Zone Totals Are Not Zone-Specific**: The `new map`, `map lookup`, `add host object`, and `Compare<HostObject>` Tracy zones aggregate across all callers — enforcing-storage, guest `map_put`/`map_get`, SAC paths, and more. A HostMap optimization must first prove the target zone is dominated by the specific call site being fixed. After T=8 division and sub-zone isolation, both lookup-index caching (for repeated `map_get`) and structural sharing (for `map_put` update chains) are below the Medium floor on soroswap. Additionally, `add_host_object` is required for every `map_put` result even with structural sharing (immutable handle semantics), so it is never removable by a representation change.

13. **SAC Dispatch Layer Cannot Remove Mandatory Frame/Conversion Work**: Cited `ScVal to Val` and `Val to ScVal` zones around `InvokeContract` are XDR-boundary costs at the top-level `HostFunction::InvokeContract` handler, not macro-dispatch overhead inside `StellarAssetContract.call`. The SAC frame arg clone is required for `require_auth` semantics. A correct specialized SAC transfer dispatch removes only symbol-scan + arity-check overhead — a micro-optimization well below Medium. Do not count conversion zones as evidence for dispatch-layer specialization savings.

14. **Storage Conversion Directions Are Distinct**: Contract-supplied storage keys flow Val→ScVal (ingress); stored ledger values flow ScVal→Val (egress). An interner or cache targeting one direction cannot claim savings from the other. Additionally, key-level interning does not remove metered ordered-map probes or insert/rebuild costs. Always separate key-ingress, value-egress, and map-structure costs before projecting impact on any storage-path optimization.

15. **Apply-Phase Per-Tx Micro-Costs Are Exhaustively Sub-Threshold**: The complete set of per-tx and per-key micro-optimizations in the transactions apply path has been surveyed and every individual one is below the 1% noise floor: addReads sub-costs (xdr_size skip, TTL probe fusion, toCxxBuf re-encoding), recordStorageChanges (rwKey linear scan, getTTLKey memoization), bundle-build loop (subSha256 hoist, newFeeEvent AssetContractInfo caching, SignatureChecker sharing), commitChangesToLedgerTxn (bulk-import API), and preParallelApplyReadOnly (`op->checkValid(forApply=true)` redundant source-account reload for Soroban single-op txs). Future single-zone per-tx hypotheses in these paths should not be proposed unless a new trace shows substantially higher zone overlap; only a combined refactor addressing several zones simultaneously could potentially reach Medium.
